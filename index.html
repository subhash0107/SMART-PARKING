Economic Loss

Why Smart Parking?

Smart Parking: The Future of Urban Mobility

Smart cities require intelligent parking solutions that save time, reduce congestion, and improve the overall driving experience. Real-time parking monitoring is one of the most practical and impactful applications of IoT (Internet of Things) technology.

Our Smart Parking System helps drivers find and reserve parking around popular destinations in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. The listed locations are starting points for local operators to verify and manage.

🎯 Key Benefits

  • ✓ Real-time parking availability updates across all monitored parking lots
  • ✓ Reduced time spent searching for spots (up to 60% time savings)
  • ✓ Lower traffic congestion and emissions (45% reduction in traffic)
  • ✓ Improved city planning and infrastructure management
  • ✓ Facility-specific hourly rates shown before reservation
  • ✓ Live status from reservations and operator-managed slot updates
